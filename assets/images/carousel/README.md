# Carousel Images Instructions

## How to Add Your School Photos

This folder should contain 5 photos that will rotate on the homepage.

### Steps:

1. **Prepare your photos:**
   - Choose 5-6 photos that showcase your school, students, and activities
   - Recommended size: 1200px wide × 600px tall (landscape orientation)
   - Or similar aspect ratio (2:1 ratio works best)
   - Format: JPG or PNG

2. **Name your photos:**
   - `slide-1.jpg`
   - `slide-2.jpg`
   - `slide-3.jpg`
   - `slide-4.jpg`
   - `slide-5.jpg`

3. **Upload to GitHub:**
   - Go to your repository on GitHub
   - Navigate to: `assets/images/carousel/`
   - Click "Add file" → "Upload files"
   - Drag and drop your 5 photos
   - Click "Commit changes"

4. **That's it!** Your photos will automatically appear in the rotating carousel on the homepage.

### Photo Ideas:
- School building exterior
- Students in classrooms learning
- Traditional/cultural activities (drumming, language lessons)
- Land-based learning activities
- Community events
- Sports and after-school programs
- Students working together

### Tips:
- Photos should be well-lit and in focus
- Show diverse activities and age groups
- Capture the spirit of your school community
- Get proper permissions for photos of students

---

## Temporary Placeholders

Until you upload your photos, the carousel will show placeholder images. The site will still work perfectly - just replace these placeholders with your real photos when ready!

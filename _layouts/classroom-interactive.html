📚 WHAT WE'RE LEARNING

📖 LANGUAGE ARTS

Grade 3/4 is when reading really takes off!

  • Comprehension - predictions, questions, connections
  • Reading stamina - longer independent reading
  • Writing - paragraphs, narratives, reports
  • Grammar - sentence structure, punctuation

🔢 MATHEMATICS

Building computational fluency and reasoning:

  • Multiplication & Division - mastering facts
  • Fractions - parts of a whole
  • Measurement - length, mass, capacity, area
  • Geometry - 2D/3D shapes, angles
  • Problem Solving - multi-step problems

🌲 SCIENCE & LAND-BASED

Western science meets traditional knowledge:

  • Habitats - studying local ecosystems
  • Forces - simple machines, physics
  • Plants & Animals - identification, life cycles
  • Seasonal Activities - land-based learning

🌍 SOCIAL STUDIES

Exploring our place in the world:

  • Ontario Communities - urban, rural, Indigenous
  • First Nations History - Oji-Cree history, treaties
  • Geographic Inquiry - mapping skills

💬 OJI-CREE LANGUAGE

Building intermediate language skills:

  • Daily vocabulary and conversation
  • Reading traditional stories
  • Writing exercises and journaling
  • Storytelling with Elders
  • Songs and cultural expressions

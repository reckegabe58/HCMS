We are excited to announce the launch of our new Henry Coaster Memorial School website!

This new platform has been designed with our community in mind, making it easier for families, students, and staff to stay connected and informed about everything happening at HCMS.

## What's New?

Our new website features:

- **Easy Navigation**: Find what you need quickly with our simplified menu structure
- **Weekly Updates**: Stay informed with our after-school program schedule updated every week
- **Classroom Pages**: Each classroom will have its own dedicated page with monthly newsletters
- **Photo Galleries**: See the amazing activities and learning happening at our school
- **Mobile-Friendly**: Access the site easily from your phone or tablet

## Cultural Connection

This website honors our commitment to celebrating Oji-Cree language, traditions, and values. The design incorporates elements inspired by our community and our school's mission: "Where our students walk in the footsteps of their ancestors to achieve their dreams."

## Stay Connected

We encourage all families to:

- Check the homepage weekly for after-school program updates
- Visit your child's classroom page for monthly newsletters
- Review the calendar for important dates and events
- Contact us through our new contact form if you have questions

Thank you for your continued support of Henry Coaster Memorial School and our students!

**Miigwech!**

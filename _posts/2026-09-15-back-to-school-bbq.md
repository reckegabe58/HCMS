We're so excited to welcome everyone back for a new school year! Join us for our **Welcome Back to School BBQ**, a fun afternoon for the whole family to kick off the year together.

## Event Details

- **When:** Friday, September 18th, 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
- **Where:** Pow Wow Grounds *(if raining, the event will move to the HCMS gym)*
- **Who:** All students, families, and community members are welcome!

## What to Expect

- 🍔 Food
- 🏰 Bouncy castle
- 🎉 Activities for kids of all ages
- 🍎 Attendance prizes
- 👋 Meet the Teachers

This is your first chance this year to meet your child's teacher, enjoy some food and fun activities together, and celebrate the start of a new school year as a school community.

Bring the whole family — we'll see you there!

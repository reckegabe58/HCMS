# How to Post News Updates

## Quick Start

1. **Upload your image** to `assets/images/`
2. **Create a new file** in this folder (`_posts`)
3. **Name it:** `YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.md` (example: `2026-01-15-winter-road-open.md`)
4. **Paste the template below** and fill in your info
5. **Commit changes** and wait 1-3 minutes

---

## Template (Copy This)

```markdown
---
layout: post
title: "Your News Title Here"
date: 2026-01-15
category: Announcements
image: /assets/images/your-image.jpg
---

Write your news story here.

Add more paragraphs by leaving a blank line between them.
```

---

## What to Change

| Field | What to Write |
|-------|---------------|
| `title:` | Your headline in quotes |
| `date:` | Today's date (must match filename) |
| `category:` | Announcements, Events, Sports, or Community |
| `image:` | /assets/images/YOUR-FILENAME.jpg |
| Content below `---` | Your news story |

---

## Filename Rules

- Use today's date: `YYYY-MM-DD`
- Use dashes instead of spaces: `winter-road-open` not `winter road open`
- End with `.md`

**Example:** `2026-01-15-school-reopens-monday.md`

---

## Category Options

- `Announcements` - General school announcements
- `Events` - Upcoming or past events
- `Sports` - Sports news and updates
- `Community` - Community-related news

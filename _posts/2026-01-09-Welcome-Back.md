Welcome back, everyone! ❄️

We were so happy to welcome our students and families back to school on **January 5th** after the winter break. It has been wonderful to see familiar faces return, hear about winter adventures, and feel the energy in our classrooms once again. Students have been settling back into routines, reconnecting with friends, and easing into new learning for the new year.

As we move forward, a few quick reminders for families: please ensure students are dressed warmly for outdoor time, including hats, mittens, snow pants, and boots. Regular attendance continues to be very important as we build learning routines and momentum after the break. We also encourage families to check backpacks daily for notices, newsletters, and any items coming home from school.

Thank you for your continued support and partnership. We are looking forward to a positive and successful winter term filled with learning, growth, and community.

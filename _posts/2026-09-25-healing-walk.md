HCMS will be closed on **Wednesday, September 30th** for the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation. Families and the whole community are invited to join the Marten Falls First Nation Healing Walk — *Keekewin Mussaywin*.

## Event Details

- **When:** Wednesday, September 30, 2026
- **Walk begins:** 11:00 AM at HCMS
- **Return to HCMS:** 12:00 PM
- **Lunch** will be served following the walk, and **door prizes** will be available.

**Everyone is welcome.**

*Every Step Together: For Healing, Hope & Community.*

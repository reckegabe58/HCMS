# How to Post News Updates
## Henry Coaster Memorial School Website

---

## STEP 1: Upload Your Image

1. Go to **github.com/reckegabe58/HCMS**
2. Click the `assets` folder
3. Click the `images` folder
4. Click **Add file** → **Upload files**
5. Drag your image or click "choose your files"
6. Click the green **Commit changes** button
7. Remember your image filename (example: `my-photo.jpg`)

---

## STEP 2: Create Your Post

1. Go back to the main repository page (github.com/reckegabe58/HCMS)
2. Click the `_posts` folder
3. Click **Add file** → **Create new file**
4. Type the filename using this format:

   `YYYY-MM-DD-your-title-here.md`

   Example: `2026-01-15-winter-road-open.md`

---

## STEP 3: Copy This Template

Paste this into your new file:

```
---
layout: post
title: "Your News Title Here"
date: 2026-01-15
category: Announcements
image: /assets/images/my-photo.jpg
---

Write your news story here.

Add more paragraphs by leaving a blank line between them.
```

---

## STEP 4: Fill In Your Information

Replace these parts:

| Field | What to Write |
|-------|---------------|
| title: | Your headline in quotes |
| date: | Today's date (must match filename) |
| category: | Announcements, Events, Sports, or Community |
| image: | /assets/images/YOUR-FILENAME.jpg |
| Content | Your news story (below the ---) |

---

## STEP 5: Save Your Post

1. Scroll down to "Commit changes"
2. Click the green **Commit changes** button
3. Wait 1-3 minutes for the website to update

---

## STEP 6: View Your Post

Go to: **reckegabe58.github.io/HCMS**

Your post will appear in the School News section!

---

## QUICK REFERENCE

### Filename Format
`YYYY-MM-DD-title-with-dashes.md`

### Date Format
`2026-01-15` (Year-Month-Day)

### Image Path Format
`/assets/images/your-image-name.jpg`

### Category Options
- Announcements
- Events
- Sports
- Community

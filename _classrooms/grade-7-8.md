**Aaniin! Welcome to Grade 7/8!**

I'm Ms. Johane, and I have the privilege of guiding our oldest students as they prepare for the next chapter of their educational journey. The Grade 7/8 years are transformative—students are developing their identities, discovering their passions, and preparing to become leaders in our community.

## Our Senior Classroom

As the oldest students in our school, Grade 7/8 students are role models and leaders. They participate in mentoring younger students, organize school events, and take on responsibilities that prepare them for high school and beyond.

Our classroom environment emphasizes:
- **Independence** - Taking ownership of learning
- **Critical Thinking** - Questioning, analyzing, and evaluating
- **Cultural Pride** - Embracing and sharing Oji-Cree identity
- **Future Planning** - Exploring paths forward

## Academic Program

### Language Arts
Advanced literacy for academic and life success:
- **Literature Studies** - Novels, poetry, drama, and Indigenous literature
- **Essay Writing** - Argumentative, expository, and analytical essays
- **Research Projects** - In-depth investigations with proper citations
- **Media Studies** - Analyzing and creating media
- **Public Speaking** - Formal presentations and advocacy

### Mathematics
Building strong foundations for high school:
- **Pre-Algebra** - Variables, equations, and algebraic thinking
- **Geometry** - Proofs, constructions, and spatial reasoning
- **Data Analysis** - Statistics, probability, and graphing
- **Financial Mathematics** - Practical math for real life
- **Problem Solving** - Complex, multi-step challenges

### Science
Rigorous scientific inquiry:
- **Chemistry** - Matter, atoms, and chemical reactions
- **Physics** - Forces, energy, and motion
- **Biology** - Cells, genetics, and human systems
- **Earth Science** - Climate, geology, and environmental issues
- **Traditional Science** - Indigenous ways of knowing the natural world

### History & Geography
Understanding our world and our place in it:
- **World History** - Ancient civilizations to modern times
- **Canadian History** - In-depth study including Indigenous perspectives
- **Geography** - Physical processes and human impact
- **Current Events** - Engaging with news and global issues
- **Civics** - Rights, responsibilities, and civic engagement

## Cultural Leadership Program

Grade 7/8 students participate in a special cultural leadership program:

- **Elder Mentorship** - Regular one-on-one time with community Elders
- **Cultural Teaching** - Learning to share knowledge with younger students
- **Language Preservation** - Advanced Oji-Cree studies and documentation
- **Ceremony Participation** - Appropriate involvement in traditional practices
- **Community Service** - Contributing to the wellbeing of our community

## High School Preparation

We actively prepare students for the transition to secondary school:

### Academic Preparation
- Study skills and note-taking strategies
- Time management and organization
- Test-taking strategies
- Independent research skills

### Social-Emotional Preparation
- Managing transitions and change
- Building resilience and coping skills
- Developing healthy relationships
- Setting and achieving goals

### Practical Preparation
- Understanding course selection
- Exploring career pathways
- Connecting with high school representatives
- Visiting potential secondary schools

## Graduation

Our Grade 8 graduation ceremony is a special celebration that honors:
- Academic achievement
- Cultural growth and participation
- Community contribution
- The transition to new beginnings

Students work throughout the year on graduation requirements and participate in planning their ceremony.

Grade 7/8 students are held to high standards:

| Area | Expectation |

I maintain open communication with families:
- **Email** - Available for questions and concerns
- **Phone** - Call the office to leave a message

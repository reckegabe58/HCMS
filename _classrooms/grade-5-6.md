Boozhoo, and welcome to our Grade 5/6 class! My name is Sam Recke-Smith. I've been at HCMS for three years as the Student Success Coordinator, and this year I'm making a switch to teach the Grade 5/6 class alongside my co-teacher, Avtar Singh.

Our class is built on the Seven Grandfather Teachings and the teaching of Mino Bimaadiisiwin. To walk the good path, we will work to create a safe space where students can be curious and confident as they continue on their learning journeys.

Our priority this year is putting land-based learning at the front of our classroom by partnering with local knowledge keepers. We'll weave the Ontario curriculum through that land-based learning and keep building our students' literacy skills along the way.

I'm excited to watch our students grow as readers, writers, mathematicians, artists and scientists as the year goes on. Our door is always open, so feel free to stop by anytime.

**Our Classroom Community**

We have a calm corner where students can take a moment to reset when they need it, and we celebrate showing up and trying hard — students earn rewards for attendance, participation and effort, and we work together toward class goals like extra time outside, a movie afternoon, and a class trip to the store.

Warmly,
*Mr. Sam*

**Aaniin! Welcome to Native Language Class!**

I'm Ms. Brenda, and I have the honour of teaching our Oji-Cree language to all students at Henry Coaster Memorial School. Language is at the heart of who we are as Oji-Cree people, and it is my privilege to help pass this gift on to our young people.

## Our Language Program

The Native Language program serves all students from Kindergarten through Grade 8. Each class visits the Language Room regularly for dedicated Oji-Cree instruction. Our program is designed to meet students where they are, whether they are beginners or already have some knowledge of the language from home.

We focus on:
- **Speaking & Listening** - Building conversational skills and understanding spoken Oji-Cree
- **Reading & Writing** - Learning to read and write using Oji-Cree syllabics and Roman orthography
- **Cultural Context** - Understanding the deep connection between language and culture
- **Everyday Use** - Practical vocabulary for daily life and communication

## What Students Learn

### Early Grades (K-2)
- Basic greetings and phrases (Aaniin, Miigwech, Baamaapii)
- Numbers and counting
- Colours and shapes
- Animal names
- Family words
- Simple songs and rhymes

### Middle Grades (3-5)
- Conversational phrases and sentences
- Seasons, weather, and nature vocabulary
- Introduction to syllabics
- Traditional stories and legends
- Longer songs and prayers
- Describing people, places, and things

### Upper Grades (6-8)
- Complex sentence structures
- Reading and writing in syllabics
- Traditional teachings and ceremonies vocabulary
- Storytelling skills
- Land-based and hunting/trapping terminology
- Preserving and sharing language with others

## Why Language Matters

Our Oji-Cree language carries within it thousands of years of knowledge, wisdom, and connection to the land. When we speak our language, we are honouring our ancestors and ensuring that future generations will know who they are.

Language revitalization is not just about learning words - it's about strengthening our identity, our community, and our connection to the Creator and the land.

## Supporting Language Learning at Home

Families play a crucial role in language learning. Here are some ways to support your child:

- Use Oji-Cree greetings at home (Aaniin in the morning, Baamaapii when leaving)
- Ask your child to teach you what they learned
- Speak with Elders and family members who know the language
- Listen to Oji-Cree music and stories together
- Label items around your home in Oji-Cree
- Celebrate when your child uses the language

## Resources

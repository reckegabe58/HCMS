**Aaniin! Welcome to Grade 1/2 Class B!**

We're Ms. Liza and Mr. Anthony, and we're excited to welcome you to our learning community. Our classroom is a place where curiosity is celebrated, mistakes are learning opportunities, and every student's voice matters.

## Our Classroom Vision

We believe that every child has unique gifts to share. Our multi-grade setting allows us to create a family-like atmosphere where students support each other's growth. We combine the Ontario curriculum with Oji-Cree teachings to create a rich, meaningful educational experience.

## Daily Rhythm

Our day follows a consistent rhythm that helps students feel secure while keeping learning engaging:

**Morning Block (8:30-10:30)**
- Morning meeting and Oji-Cree greeting
- Literacy instruction and guided reading
- Writing workshop

**Mid-Day Block (10:30-12:00)**
- Outdoor learning/recess
- Mathematics instruction
- Number games and problem-solving

**Afternoon Block (12:45-3:00)**
- Science/Social Studies exploration
- Oji-Cree language time
- Creative arts and cultural activities
- Closing circle and reflection

## Learning Goals This Year

### Reading
By the end of the year, Grade 1 students will be reading simple chapter books, while Grade 2 students will read fluently with comprehension. We focus on:
- Decoding strategies
- Reading comprehension
- Building vocabulary
- Developing a love of reading

### Writing
Students will learn to express their ideas clearly through:
- Story writing
- Journal entries
- Letters and cards
- Oji-Cree writing activities

### Math
We use manipulatives, games, and real-world problems to develop:
- Number sense and counting
- Addition and subtraction fluency
- Measurement and data
- Shapes and spatial reasoning

## How We Celebrate Learning

- **Star of the Week** - Each student gets a special week to share about themselves
- **Celebration of Learning** - Monthly events where families can see student work
- **Cultural Celebrations** - Participating in traditional ceremonies and events

## Family Involvement

We love having families involved in our classroom! Here are some ways you can participate:
- Volunteer to read with students
- Share a traditional skill or story
- Help with classroom events
- Support homework at home
